# Competitor and market research field catalog

Select fields that affect the decision; do not force every field into every study. Keep the user's requested fields first.

## Identity and commercial status

- Company, brand, product, model, generation, predecessor/successor
- Category, subcategory, form factor, architecture
- Announcement date, launch date, preorder date, on-sale date, first delivery date
- Current status: concept, prototype, announced, preorder, shipping, generally available, region-limited, discontinued
- Geography, language, platform and regulatory availability
- Launch price, current list price, street price, subscription, accessories, installation, financing, subsidy, tax
- Three-year total cost of ownership, warranty, repairability, service life

## Functions and customer value

- Core job-to-be-done and differentiated promise
- Primary and secondary functions
- AI modalities: text, voice, image, video, sensor, agent/action execution
- Content creation, communication, productivity, entertainment, navigation, commerce, accessibility, health, enterprise workflows
- Offline capability, latency, memory/context, supported models, model switching, developer APIs
- Customer segment, buyer, user, use frequency, purchase trigger, switching cost
- Major scenarios, unsuitable scenarios, failure modes

## Hardware and technical specifications

- Dimensions, weight, weight distribution, materials, ergonomics, fit, prescription support
- Processor, memory, storage, sensors, cameras, audio, microphones
- Display type, resolution, FOV, PPD, brightness, refresh rate, eye box, transparency, color, optical architecture
- Battery capacity; standby, mixed use, continuous audio, call, recording, display-on, and case-total endurance
- Charging time, fast charging, replaceable battery, charging case, wired operation
- Connectivity, tethering, supported hosts, operating system, compatibility
- Durability, IP rating, operating temperature, privacy indicators, security hardware
- Measured performance versus vendor-claimed performance

## Interaction and experience

- Voice, wake word, buttons, touchpad, dial, ring, watch, gesture, hand tracking, eye tracking, head motion, EMG, phone, keyboard/controller
- Setup, pairing, calibration, task steps, discoverability, accessibility
- Response time, task success rate, false activation, error recovery
- Public acceptability, noise, light leakage, audio leakage, privacy and bystander signaling
- App quality, updates, account requirement, regional restrictions

## Market and business

- TAM/SAM/SOM with definition, geography, year and methodology
- Shipment, sell-in, sell-through, activation, installed base, orders, backers, revenue
- Market share, growth, ASP, price band, channel share
- Units, currency, exchange-rate date, nominal/real values
- Business model: hardware margin, subscription, advertising, commerce, services, data, enterprise licensing
- Distribution: direct, marketplace, retail, optical, carrier, reseller, enterprise integrator
- Partnerships, supply chain, production capacity, patents, standards, certifications
- Funding, profitability, recurring revenue, customer acquisition and retention when available

## Competition and strategy

- Direct competitor, indirect competitor, substitute, complement, benchmark, prototype
- Strengths, weaknesses, moat, dependency, lock-in, ecosystem leverage
- Product cadence, geographic expansion, channel reach, brand advantage
- Adoption evidence, reviews, returns, retention, developer activity
- Privacy, safety, legal, regulatory, ethical and reputational risk
- White space, unmet needs, disruption vectors, likely responses

## Evidence metadata

For every important value retain:

- Raw claim and normalized value
- Unit and test conditions
- Geography and customer/channel scope
- Event date and publication/retrieval date
- Source title, URL, publisher and source tier
- Vendor claim, independent measurement, estimate, or inference
- Confidence: high, medium, low
- Conflict or caveat note

## Common non-comparable metrics

- Shipments vs retail sales vs activated devices vs installed base
- Revenue vs GMV vs bookings vs funding
- List price vs subsidized price vs street price vs total ownership cost
- Standby vs mixed use vs continuous workload vs charging-case total
- Peak vs sustained performance
- Lab result vs field test
- Global vs country vs online-only channel
- Announcement vs launch vs first shipment
- App downloads vs active users
- Crowdfunding backers vs delivered units
